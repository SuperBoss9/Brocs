Brocs works entirely on your device.

• It does not send browsing history, URLs, hostnames, IP addresses, or proxy settings to any server.
• It does not use analytics, advertising, or telemetry.
• It does not call external “what is my IP” or geolocation services.
• It does not change your proxy configuration (read-only).
• Temporary connection state is kept only in the browser for the extension UI.

No browsing or network information is transmitted anywhere.
